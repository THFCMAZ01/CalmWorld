About Calm World

This website exists because mental health is a global need and calm should be free.

Over 1 billion people worldwide experience stress or anxiety. Many have no access to paid apps, therapy, or classes. Calm World collects the simplest, most universal mindfulness tools from every continent and makes them available to anyone with an internet connection — in one quiet place.
