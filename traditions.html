Mindfulness Traditions From Six Continents

JapanForest Bathing (Shinrin-yoku)

Spend time among trees, breathing deeply. Proven to lower stress hormones.

IndiaNadi Shodhana (Alternate Nostril Breathing)

Balances the nervous system in under two minutes.

BrazilEarthing

Walk barefoot on grass or sand to reconnect with the Earth's energy.

ScandinaviaHygge Mindfulness

Light a candle, wrap in a blanket, and fully enjoy the moment of cosiness.

MoroccoMint Tea Ritual

Brew and drink mint tea slowly, making the simple act a meditation.

Tibet / GlobalLoving-Kindness (Metta)

Send silent wishes of peace to yourself, loved ones, and all beings everywhere.
