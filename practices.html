10 Daily Practices You Can Do Anywhere

1. 4-7-8 Breathing

Inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 7, exhale for 8. Repeat four times.

2. One-Minute Body Scan

Close your eyes and slowly notice each part of your body from toes to head.

3. Gratitude Pause

Name three things you're thankful for right now.

4. Five-Finger Grounding

Look for 5 things you can see, 4 you can touch, 3 you can hear, 2 you can smell, 1 you can taste.

5. Walking Meditation

Walk slowly, feeling each foot touch the ground.

6. Loving-Kindness Phrases

Silently repeat: "May I be safe. May I be healthy. May I live with ease."

7. Sky Gazing

Look up at the sky or ceiling for 60 seconds and let thoughts drift like clouds.

8. Shoulder Roll & Neck Stretch

Gently roll shoulders and tilt head side to side for 30 seconds.

9. Hand-on-Heart

Place one hand on your heart, breathe deeply, and feel warmth.

10. Smile for No Reason

Gently smile for 15 seconds — the brain responds as if you're happy.
